CampusNest is a student residence in Hamra, Beirut, a 5-minute walk from the AUB Main Gate. Opened in 2023, the building has 6 floors, an elevator and 48 furnished rooms, and it welcomes students from any university in Beirut. Our mission is to give students a safe, affordable and all-inclusive place to live, so they can focus on their studies instead of bills, power cuts and landlords.

We offer three types of furnished rooms: a shared room for two students at $350 per student per month, a single room at $500 per month, and a studio with a private bathroom and kitchenette at $700 per month. CampusNest is a mixed building with separate floors: floors 2 and 3 are for female students, floors 4 and 5 are for male students, and the studios on floor 6 are open to all. The rent includes everything: 24/7 electricity with generator and solar backup, water, fiber Wi-Fi, weekly cleaning, free laundry, floor kitchens, a 24/7 study room, a rooftop lounge, and round-the-clock security. Contracts run one semester at a time, with move-in from September 1 for Fall and February 1 for Spring, and a security deposit equal to one month's rent. The only discount is 5% off for paying the full semester upfront.

What makes CampusNest different is simple: no power cuts, no surprise bills, no 12-month lease, and a short walk to class. The residence is run by co-founders Karim Haddad (operations) and Yasmine Khoury (student life), with building manager Antoine Saliba on site every day. Rooms are assigned first-come, first-served after a viewing, and only the CampusNest office can confirm whether a room is available. Viewings can be booked Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 18:00. Booking is simple: view the room, fill in a short application, sign the contract, and pay the first month's rent plus the deposit.
